# Dashboard patterns worth adding to UseRight

Research date: 2026-11-03. Sources below are official product docs/help pages; I read each linked page. “Why it works” is a design inference from the documented interaction, not a claim that I user-tested the product. Effort and ranking are guesses for this codebase and the Nov 9 cutoff.

## Patterns table

| Pattern | Who uses it (source) | Why it works | Do we have it? | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|

([PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/us/business/operations/reporting-tools?locale.x=us_US), [Mercury](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/38790547830036-Viewing-cashflow-and-transactions-data-on-your-Transactions-page)) | PayPal Business, Mercury | **Inference:** “this month” and “all time” answer different questions. Naming the period keeps totals interpretable; matching the chart/table filters prevents two views from disagreeing. | **Partial.** Overview clearly separates earned, pending and refunded, and demo from live money; it has no date range, so summary totals are lifetime. Ledger has status/type filters but no visible date-range control. (`docs/ui/overview.md`, `docs/ui/ledger.md`) | M (guess) |
| **Money summaries drill into the records behind them.** Ramp describes clickable charts that drill from a spike to exact transactions; Stripe Dashboard search groups matches and lets users open result tables. ([Ramp](https://ramp.com/reporting), [Stripe](https://docs.stripe.com/dashboard/search)) | Ramp, Stripe | **Inference:** an amount becomes easier to trust and act on when one click reveals the transactions that make it up. | **Partial.** Overview’s unpaid rows and recent activity link to deals/ledger; the three Earned / Waiting / Refunded totals themselves are static. ([Lemon Squeezy](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/products/subscriptions)) | Lemon Squeezy | **Inference:** words alone are insufficient when “cancelled” may still mean access through a paid period; say the consequence and next action with the state. | **Mostly.** UseRight has status tips, progress, and receipts with end/renewal dates. ## Ranked additions for Nov 9

Ranked by **expected judge/demo value per effort**; impact and effort are estimates, not measured results.

1. **Make the three Overview totals clickable into their source records.** Earned and Refunded should open the matching Ledger view; Waiting for payment should open the unpaid invoice queue. Preserve current labels and demo separation. This is the smallest visible trust win: the judge can verify a total in one click. **High impact / S–M effort (guess).** Ramp’s chart-to-transaction drill-down is the model. ([Ramp](https://ramp.com/reporting))
2. **Add exact ID search to Cmd/Ctrl+K.** Search brief ID, PayPal invoice ID, capture ID and receipt ID in addition to the existing client/photo/status terms; show the record type and status in each result. Reuse existing read models and keep the search read-only. This helps find the live payment quickly during the demo. **High / S–M (guess).** Stripe searches across payments, invoices and payouts and groups its results. ([Stripe](https://docs.stripe.com/dashboard/search))
3. **Put oldest “Needs you” request first and show “waiting since” from its server `createdAt`.** Keep the current chips and table; add an explicit sort control or default ordering only in the Needs you view. Avoid claiming a due date until the server has one. **High / S (guess).** Mercury and Linear separate incoming work that needs a decision from completed work. ([Mercury](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28768945847316-Bill-Pay-overview), [Linear](https://linear.app/docs/triage))
4. **Add clear time presets to Overview and Ledger: “All time,” “This month,” and “Last 30 days.”** The API must calculate each amount for the chosen interval; label the selection beside the totals and keep pending invoices distinct from collected payments. **High / M (guess).** PayPal and Mercury make time range and transaction filters first-class. ([PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/us/business/operations/reporting-tools?locale.x=us_US), [Mercury](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/38790547830036-Viewing-cashflow-and-transactions-data-on-your-Transactions-page))
5. **Add an “Expiring / renewal due” block to Overview only if the Nov 9 build has reliable renewal dates and actions.** Show photo/client, licence end date, current state, and one correct next step (draft renewal vs auto-renewal already scheduled). If the data/action is not ready, keep it out of the demo. **Medium-high / M (guess; data readiness unknown).** Lemon Squeezy’s documented lifecycle makes “cancelled but still valid” distinct from “expired.” ([Lemon Squeezy](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/products/subscriptions))

Do not replace the existing deals table with a chat assistant: the dashboard already has structured approval, status and evidence patterns. The opportunity is to make each money/status item easier to locate, verify and act on. **This is a recommendation/inference**, grounded in the local dashboard sheets (`docs/ui/README.md`, `overview.md`, `deals.md`, `ledger.md`, `deal-room.md`, `ask.md`) and the linked product docs.
